# Employee Handbook

## Working hours

The standard working day is eight hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday through Friday. Teams may agree on different schedules with their manager.

## Leave

Employees receive 25 paid vacation days per calendar year. Vacation requests should normally be submitted at least two weeks before the first day of leave. The manager approves requests in the HR system.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to team requirements. New remote-work arrangements must be agreed with the employee's manager.
