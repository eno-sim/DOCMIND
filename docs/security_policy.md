# Information Security Policy

## Passwords

Employees must use passwords with at least 12 characters. Passwords must not be reused across company systems. Multi-factor authentication is required for production systems and administrative accounts.

## Incidents

Security incidents must be reported to security@example.com within one hour of discovery. The security team acknowledges reports within four business hours and records each incident in the incident register.

## Data handling

Confidential documents may be stored only in approved company storage. Public links must not be created for confidential documents.
