# Project Alpha

Project Alpha is the company's customer analytics platform.

## Schedule

The design review is scheduled for 14 March 2025. The public beta begins on 2 April 2025. The production launch is planned for 19 May 2025.

## Ownership

Elena Rossi is the project owner. David Chen is the engineering lead. The project has a budget of 240,000 euros.

## Support

The Alpha team provides business-hours support from Monday through Friday. Critical production incidents are escalated to the engineering lead.
