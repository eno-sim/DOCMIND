# Project Beta

Project Beta is an internal document search application.

## Schedule

The design review is scheduled for 21 March 2025. The public beta begins on 15 April 2025. The production launch is planned for 30 June 2025.

## Ownership

Marco Bianchi is the project owner. Priya Shah is the engineering lead. The project has a budget of 180,000 euros.

## Support

Beta is available to internal users only until the production launch. Issues should be reported through the internal service desk.
