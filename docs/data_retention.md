# Data Retention Standard

## Customer data

Customer event data is retained for 24 months after collection. After the retention period, automated deletion removes the data from primary storage within seven days.

## Backups

Encrypted backups are retained for 90 days. Backup deletion is handled by the infrastructure team during the weekly maintenance window.

## Exceptions

A legal hold suspends deletion for the affected records. Legal holds are created by the legal team and remain active until formally released.
